Mission: CAN plan to build and launch
Description: The Cooperative Action Network (CAN) is a membership organization that gives ordinary people real political power through coordinated action. Members receive access to legitimate, trustworthy information about important issues—developed through citizen assemblies—and take simple weekly actions that add up to major collective impact. CAN harnesses the coordinated spending power of its members to reward responsible companies and pressure irresponsible ones, turning everyday consumer choices into a powerful force for change. CAN operates with transparent governance that keeps power distributed and accountable, ensuring no single faction can capture the network. The system is designed with strong privacy protections and operational security to safeguard participants, especially those in repressive countries where civic action carries risk. Members can participate pseudonymously through zero-knowledge credentials, and the infrastructure is resilient against surveillance, coercion, and blocking attempts. Edit |
