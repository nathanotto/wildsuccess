Operating principles

Twelve tenets that shape how Wild Success thinks about its members and the work the product is in service of. Voice, microcopy, and feature decisions all derive from these.

  1. Fears of failure are planning constraints.Information about what to plan around — not a flaw to overcome.
  2. Every human wants to contribute and be acknowledged.Design for visible contribution.
  3. Primary data for truth; grounded assessment for action.Show the real thing before the summary.
  4. Repetitions become systems.Repeated failure → prevention. Repeated value → solved problem.
  5. People need agency like they need air.No paternalism. No silent system actions.
  6. Growth comes from value, cooperation, and alignment.Growth is a consequence — not a metric to optimize.
  7. Defection is detected and corrected immediately.Disputes and missed expectations surface fast.
  8. Reputation is the recent accumulation of experience with you.Earned in interactions, not declared in profiles.
  9. Agree on values, differ on vision; agree on vision, differ on plans; agree on plans, differ on actions.Disagreement on tactics is normal and healthy.
  10. Differences below values alignment do not impair connection.We each take action according to our lights.
  11. Real-time, trustable data is essential.Stale or guessed data corrodes the system.
  12. Kind, objective feedback is a gift — given and accepted.Feedback flows are first-class flows.
